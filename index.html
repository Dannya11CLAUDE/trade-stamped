Our promise

More than a five-star rating.

A rating alone rarely tells the full story. Trade Stamped aims to give people context: what work was done, how the job went and what the customer wants others to know.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

A few things we're making clear from day one.

Is Trade Stamped live yet?

Not yet. This is a front-end prototype. The live directory, secure accounts, shared database, review submissions and moderation tools still need to be built and deployed.

Are the tradespeople and reviews shown real?

No. All profile names, ratings and snippets in this prototype are fictional demonstration content. They are not endorsements and should be replaced with genuine, permissioned information before public launch.

What does “verified” mean?

A profile should only show a verification status when Trade Stamped has completed a defined check. The label must explain what was checked and should not imply that every aspect of a business or job is guaranteed.

Can a tradesperson respond to a review?

That is a planned feature. The live platform should give tradespeople a fair right of reply and offer a way to report content that may be fake, abusive or unlawful.

Will customers have to pay?

The starting plan is for customers to search and read reviews for free. Any future paid services for tradespeople would be explained clearly before launch.

Do these forms send information to Trade Stamped?

No. In this demo, form entries are saved only in this browser's local storage. They are not sent to us or shared with anyone. The live site will need a secure server, privacy information and suitable consent before collecting personal information.

Privacy overview

Draft notes for the prototype — not a final privacy policy.

This prototype runs in your browser. The interest, review and feature suggestion forms store entries in browser local storage on this device only. They are not transmitted to Trade Stamped or another server.

Before public launch

  • Identify the legal entity responsible for the service and publish contact details.
  • Explain what personal data is collected, why it is needed, the lawful basis, retention period and any service providers.
  • Provide a way to exercise data rights and contact the responsible organisation.
  • Assess cookies, analytics, security, data access and deletion requirements.
  • Do not collect real customer reviews until secure storage, moderation and privacy processes are in place.

This draft is not legal advice and must be reviewed and completed before the site accepts real users.

Review rules

Proposed community standards for a future live platform.

  • Only describe a genuine experience you personally had with the business.
  • Be specific, factual and fair; separate opinion from claims of fact.
  • Do not post private contact details, addresses, payment information or personal data about other people.
  • No threats, harassment, discriminatory abuse, spam, fake reviews or reviews written in exchange for undisclosed incentives.
  • Tradespeople should have a fair right to reply and a clear route to report or challenge content.
  • Trade Stamped should review reports consistently, keep records of moderation decisions and remove content where legally required.

These are proposed rules for planning purposes. A live service needs a documented moderation and appeals process before public reviews open.
